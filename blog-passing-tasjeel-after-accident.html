Why Cars Fail Tasjeel & Shamil Inspection

In the UAE, vehicle safety testing centers like Tasjeel (Sharjah & Dubai) and Shamil use rigorous checks to inspect a car's underside frame. Any visual cracking, improper structural welding, or frame rail sway over factory limits will trigger an immediate test failure tag.

How We Restore Failed Vehicles

At Drive Zone Auto Main in Industrial Area 12, Sharjah, we take your failed test sheet, mount the car onto our CAR-O-LINER 3D electronic rack, and pull the frame back into exact manufacturer specs before refinishing protective undercoating.
