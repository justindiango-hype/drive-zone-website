The Gold Standard in Frame Alignment

A vehicle's chassis is its structural foundation. When involved in a collision, even a minor one, the frame can shift out of alignment. Driving a vehicle with a compromised chassis is not only dangerous—affecting suspension, tire wear, and airbag deployment—but it will also cause the vehicle to fail mandatory UAE safety inspections.

At Drive Zone Auto Main in Sharjah Industrial Area 12, we utilize world-class CAR-O-LINER 3D Computerized Measuring Technology. This advanced system allows us to detect invisible structural damage by comparing your car’s exact frame dimensions to the original manufacturer's blueprints down to the millimeter.

4 Signs Your Car Needs Chassis Repair

1

Uneven Tire Wear

If your tires are wearing down rapidly on one side, your frame may be bent, throwing off your wheel alignment permanently.

2

Vehicle "Crabbing"

When driving straight, the rear of your car seems to track slightly to the left or right of the front wheels.

3

Doors or Hood Won't Close

Uneven panel gaps or doors that require force to latch indicate the structural cage has shifted.

4

Recent Accident History

Even low-speed fender benders can tweak the unibody. A proactive 3D measurement prevents future safety issues.

Our 3-Step Measuring & Alignment Process

  • 01

    Electronic Diagnostic Scan

    We place your vehicle on the CAR-O-LINER bench. Laser sensors measure reference points underneath the vehicle and cross-reference them with a global database of over 17,000 vehicle blueprints to identify exact deviation points.

  • 02

    Precision Hydraulic Pulling

    Using multi-directional hydraulic towers, our master technicians gently pull and push the high-strength steel or aluminum frame back into its exact factory position without compromising metal integrity.

  • 03

    Final Verification & Reporting

    We conduct a secondary 3D scan to verify structural perfection. You receive a detailed printout proving the chassis has been restored to factory dimensions.
