Our Refinishing Process

  • 01

    Surface Prep & Dent Removal

    The foundation of a great paint job is flawless metalwork. We carefully pull dents, fill deep scratches, and sand the body down to a completely smooth surface.

  • 02

    Priming & Masking

    We apply high-grade automotive primer to protect the metal and ensure paint adhesion. Trim, glass, and wheels are meticulously masked off to prevent overspray.

  • 03

    Paint & Clear Coat Baking

    Base color and UV-resistant clear coats are applied inside our heated booth. The oven baking process cures the paint instantly for a mirror-like, durable shine.
