Why Do Vehicles Fail Tasjeel & Shamil Chassis Tests?

UAE registration centers like Tasjeel (Sharjah & Dubai) and Shamil use laser measuring tools and underbody inspections. If your car has been in a past accident, even minor frame distortions or improper structural welding will result in a REJECTED safety report.

1. Frame Rail Deviation

Unibody frame rails that are twisted or swayed out of OEM millimeter tolerances.

2. Uncertified Welds

Poor structural welding or unpolished joint repairs from unqualified roadside garages.

3. Subframe & Suspension Stress

Shifted shock towers or subframe crossmembers preventing correct wheel alignment.

Our 3-Step Tasjeel Passing Process

  1. Laser Diagnostic Scan: We mount your car on our CAR-O-LINER bench to pinpoint the exact millimeter deviation noted on your Tasjeel failure sheet.
  2. Hydraulic Frame Pulling: Our technicians pull the steel frame back to exact original manufacturer geometry using electronic 3D monitoring.
  3. Inspection Certificate & Passing Guarantee: We re-check all measurements before you return to Tasjeel, guaranteeing a 100% successful re-test pass.
